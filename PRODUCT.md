# Antenne: product one-pager

**What it is:** Fleet launches, features, and updates as a news feed

**Who it is for:** The fleet's own visitors and its operator. A visitor lands on
any neorgon.com site and has no way to know what shipped this week; the operator
ships constantly and has no channel that announces it without hand-writing HTML.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A corner popup on the hub catches you between tools, late evening
on a desktop: three fresh headlines, one click to the full story, one click to
dismiss. The desk half happens earlier the same evening, localhost only,
reading drafts over coffee and spiking the weak ones.

**What done looks like:** A visitor closes the popup knowing what changed this
week, or the operator publishes an approved batch and the feed, the RSS, and the
hub popup all update from one committed JSON file.
